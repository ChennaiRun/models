# Vehicles

Shared Chennai public-transport vehicles, reusable in any area scene. Every asset is original procedural geometry generated by a script in `tools/`. No existing openly licensed, downloadable model of these vehicles was found (search notes in [`REFERENCE_MEDIA.md`](REFERENCE_MEDIA.md)).

| Folder | Vehicle | Size | Triangles |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`mtc_bus/`](mtc_bus/) | MTC 12 m Ashok Leyland low-floor city bus, 2024-25 purple/cyan livery, route 21G Tambaram | 12.0 × 2.55 × 3.19 m | ~2.3k |
| [`chennai_emu/`](chennai_emu/) | Chennai Suburban 9-car ICF WAU-4 EMU, cream-and-green livery | 192.6 m over bodies (193.7 m over buffers), 3.66 m wide, 3.88 m roof | ~26k |
| [`cmrl_metro/`](cmrl_metro/) | Chennai Metro Phase 1 Alstom Metropolis, 4 cars (DMC1-TC1-TC2-DMC2), stainless with blue cab and stripes | 89.1 m over bodies, 2.9 m wide, 3.9 m roof | ~12k |

Each folder holds the `.glb` and a `.json` with dimensions, consist, node names, sources, approximations and geometry stats.

## Coordinate frame

These assets are vehicle-local and are not georeferenced.

- glTF metres, +Y up.
- +Z is the vehicle front (the bus front, or the leading cab of a train). +X is the vehicle's left side, which is the kerb/door side for left-hand traffic.
- y = 0 is the road surface (bus) or the top of rail (trains).
- The origin is at the vehicle or train centre, on the track centreline for trains.
- Track gauges are 1.676 m broad gauge for the EMU and 1.435 m standard gauge for the metro.

Each coach or car is its own child node (for example `coach_01_MC` or `car_DMC1`), placed at its centre, so a scene can split a rake or animate individual cars. Each car has one `COL_*` box collider. Hide those renderers in Unity. The bus wheels are a separate `wheels` node.

Placing a vehicle in a georeferenced v2 area (x=east, y=up, z=-north) needs a yaw about +Y and a translation. For example, the Chrompet rail heading of 16.5° east of north means a yaw that turns +Z toward that heading.

## Regenerating

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install trimesh numpy pillow
# Tamil text on the bus needs libraqm (macOS: brew install libraqm; Debian: apt install libraqm0)
DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH=/opt/homebrew/lib .venv/bin/python vehicles/tools/mtc_bus.py
.venv/bin/python vehicles/tools/chennai_emu.py
.venv/bin/python vehicles/tools/cmrl_metro.py
```

The scripts run from the repository root and are deterministic: two consecutive runs produce byte-identical GLBs. Without libraqm, the bus generator prints a warning and falls back to English side lettering, because Pillow cannot shape Tamil correctly without it. The metadata field `tamil_text_shaped` records which version was built.

`tools/preview.py <glb> <out.png> [--front M]` renders six orthographic inspection views with a small z-buffer rasteriser, so no Blender is needed. Add `--hero file.png` for an anti-aliased 3/4 still, `--gif file.gif` for a 36-frame turntable, and `--only coach_01,coach_02` to show whole cars only.

Ready-made previews are in [`previews/`](previews/): a `*_hero.png` still, a `*_turntable.gif` and a `*_views*.png` six-view sheet for each vehicle. The train stills and GIFs show the leading two cars. The `*_views_full.png` sheets show the whole train.

## Validation (2026-10-31)

- `python3 scripts/validate_repo.py`: all three GLBs parse, with embedded textures and no external resources.
- Each generator was run twice with identical SHA-1 output.
- Front, rear, both sides and 3/4 views were inspected visually. Textures checked: the bus LED boards, number plates and Tamil operator text; the EMU cab V-livery, destination board and unit number; the metro cab face and car numbers.

## Limitations

- These are mid-detail exterior models with no interiors. Glazing is opaque dark glass.
- Dimensions are typical published values for each vehicle class, not taken from manufacturer drawings. See `approximations` in each JSON.
- Not represented: the older brown-cream and blue MTC buses, the stainless MEDHA and AC EMUs, and the Phase 2 driverless metro trains. Each can be added as a parameter or a sibling generator.
- Unity integration is still outstanding. The Chromepet sync/build scripts do not import `vehicles/` yet.

Player models remain in [`../players/`](../players/).
