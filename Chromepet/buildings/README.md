# buildings
OSM footprints extruded to estimated heights (type-based guesses; OSM has almost no height data here). Local metres, X=east, Z=north, Y=up, origin 12.9487 N 80.1394 E.

## v2
`campus_buildings.glb`: 60 OSM campus footprints styled per video notes (Admin blue glass + portico + Kalam statue, LHC pale yellow-green with sunshades, light-green dept blocks, red/yellow Aero with portico, Rajam Hall white/orange, hostels pink/purple, pale-yellow/blue, green/pink, Hangar I rusty pitched-roof shed, Hangar II vaulted roof, etc.), 3.5 m storeys, flat roofs. Names with "(approx)" were assigned heuristically to untagged footprints; see `../data/v2_meta.json`.
`outside_buildings.glb`: 8,732 OSM footprints, 2-6 storeys (1-3 near the station), pastel walls, parapets, shop awnings near the station.

## detailed/
Hand/bpy-modelled buildings that replace procedural boxes (Unity scene builder imports them, uses `COL_*` nodes as invisible MeshColliders and disables the replaced procedural node).
* `detailed/ct_charles_babbage.glb` – Charles Babbage Block / Dept. of Computer Technology, from two user photos. 44 x 10.4 m, 2 storeys (3.8 m) + parapet, return wing at the right end, portico with sign, teak lattice entrance, 5 granite steps, 1:10 ramp with railings, hex-paver forecourt, potted plants. Generated by `../tools/ct_charles_babbage_bpy.py` (`blender -b -P tools/ct_charles_babbage_bpy.py` from `Chromepet/`). Placed on the footprint previously styled "Charles Babbage Block (approx)" (centre ENU 249.45, 167.68; entrance facing the avenue). Dimensions are estimates.
* `detailed/production_technology_frontage.glb` – Department of Production Technology entrance frontage from the user's Drive photos 14/45.jpg, generated by `../tools/production_technology_frontage.py` (trimesh, no Blender): blue department sign (original text rendering), square-grid jali band, oval-hole lattice screens, cream + mustard facade, maroon doors, tiled porch, `COL_frontage` collider. ORIGIN-CENTRED additive overlay (not a replacement): it still needs a placement entry in the scene builder at the Production Technology node; until then the builder's detailed auto-load would place it at the world origin. See `../data/production_frontage_meta.json`.
