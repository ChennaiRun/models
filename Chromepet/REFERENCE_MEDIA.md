# Chrompet browser and image references

These references were used for observation only. The shipped meshes are original low-poly interpretations; no Google imagery or third-party photo pixels are included in the models.

## Browser/map research

* [Madras Institute of Technology, Anna University on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Madras+Institute+of+Technology,+Anna+University/@12.9483806,80.1370933,17z/) – campus placement, main gate and surrounding road context. Google imagery is not redistributed, cached, traced or used for photogrammetry.
* [Chrompet Railway Station on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Chrompet+Railway+Station/@12.9511039,80.1406143,17z/) – station position, Station Road relationship and approximate rail alignment.

## Reusable visual references

* [Chromepet railway station (June 2025)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chromepet_railway_station_(June_2025).jpg), Sameer2905, CC BY-SA 4.0 – current yellow trilingual nameboard, black/yellow posts and platform setting.
* [ChennaiSuburbanRailway Chromepet](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ChennaiSuburbanRailway_Chromepet.jpg), Rasnaboy, CC BY-SA 3.0 – cream/maroon station-office massing, platform relationship and footbridge context.

Attribution applies to the visual references. `props/chrompet_identity.glb` is procedural source code output and does not embed either photograph.
